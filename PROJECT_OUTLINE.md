# Project Outline

## Purpose

This repository builds a reproducible, bias-resistant daily OHLCV research
pipeline for five-session directional and magnitude forecasts on US stocks and
ETFs. The decision is made after completed session `D0`, entry is at `D1` open,
and exit is at `D5` close. The project may eventually produce an underlying
forecast, uncertainty, rank, and abstention reason; it does not select options
or establish options profitability.

V2 retains the legacy research question but not the legacy implementation or
authority. No legacy source, model, trial, approval, path, or generated file
becomes active merely because it existed in the earlier project.

## Active free-only pathway

The current project path uses completely free sources and services, local
desktop storage, and GitHub backup only. It excludes paid data, subscriptions,
commercial trial offers, cloud registries, and additional hosted
infrastructure. While no complete free effective-event and delisting backend
satisfies the binding evidence contract, the project remains limited to
synthetic mechanics, legacy discovery-only evidence, and bounded prospective
raw evidence.

This cost constraint does not weaken the scientific contract. Production
inputs, outcome access, real-trial registration, training, evaluation,
candidate sealing, source activation, and production-readiness claims remain
blocked until complete free evidence satisfies every existing capability and
each controlled action receives its own authorization.

## Durable boundaries

- Research remains separate from live use and trading.
- Accepted releases are immutable; corrections create successors.
- Legacy data is read-only forensic evidence and never a runtime fallback.
- Feature, outcome, prediction, evaluation, and monitoring artifacts stay
  isolated.
- Historical discovery and prospective confirmation are distinct evidence.
- Stock/ETF and long/short sleeves are independently binding; aggregate results
  cannot hide an absent, failed, or underpowered sleeve.

The binding detail lives in
[`docs/REBUILD_CONSTITUTION.md`](docs/REBUILD_CONSTITUTION.md) and
[`docs/HISTORICAL_RESEARCH_HARNESS.md`](docs/HISTORICAL_RESEARCH_HARNESS.md).

## Enduring roadmap

1. Qualify sources and publish immutable as-received evidence.
2. Build causal identity, action, calendar, eligible-universe, feature, and
   outcome releases separately.
3. Register a counted hypothesis and chronological walk-forward design before
   outcome access.
4. Evaluate through isolated fold-local fitting and the independent four-sleeve
   gates.
5. Seal any qualifying candidate only through its separate gate.
6. Collect append-only prospective predictions, mature outcomes separately, and
   assess at the fixed end rule.
7. Consider monitored manual decision support only after prospective,
   operational, and cutover gates pass.

Documentation of this roadmap is not permission to execute a phase.
Under the active free-only pathway, steps 2 through 7 remain a conditional
future route rather than a claim that suitable evidence currently exists.
